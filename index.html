Espresso Martini

Coupe

Ingredients

  • 2oz Vodka
  • 1oz Espresso
  • 1/2oz Coffee Liqueur
  • 1/2oz Simple Syrup

Instructions

  1. In a cocktail shaker, combine the vodka, espresso, coffee liqueur, and simple syrup.
  2. Add ice and shake very hard to chill the cocktail and aerate the ingredients (this makes the foam on top of the cocktail).

Manhattan

Coupe

Ingredients

  • 2oz Rye
  • 1oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Instructions

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker about three-quarters full of ice and add the rye, vermouth, and bitters. Stir with a bar spoon until the contents are ice cold.
  2. Strain liquid into two cocktail glasses and garnish each with a lemon twist.

Rocket Pop

Highball

Ingredients

  • 1oz Grenadine
  • 1/2oz Silver Rum
  • 1/2oz Malibu
  • 1/2oz Blue Curacao
  • 1/2oz Coconut Cream

Instructions

  1. Add the blue curacao and coconut rum into a glass and stir thoughtfully.
  2. In a second glass, add the white rum and coconut cream together and stir as well.
  3. Pour the grenadine into the bottom of a highball glass.
  4. Fill the highball glass with crushed ice.
  5. Slowly pour the rum and cream of coconut mixture on top of the crushed ice.
  6. After that settles, pour the blue curacao and coconut rum mixture onto the crushed ice to fill the glass.

Long Island Iced Tea

Highball

Ingredients

  • 1/2oz Vodka
  • 1/2oz Silver Rum
  • 1/2oz Tequila
  • 1/2oz Gin
  • 1/2oz Triple Sec
  • 3/4oz Simple Syrup
  • 3/4oz Lemon Juice
  • 1/2oz Coke

Instructions

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
  2. Add the vodka, rum, tequila, gin, triple sec, lemon juice, and simple syrup.
  3. Shake well for about 10 to 15 seconds until chilled.
  4. Strain the mixture into a tall glass (like a Collins or highball glass) filled with fresh ice.
  5. Top with a splash of cola to give the drink its characteristic iced-tea color.
  6. Stir gently, garnish with a lemon slice, and serve.
