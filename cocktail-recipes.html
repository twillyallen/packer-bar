Rocket Pop

Ingredients

  • 1oz Grenadine
  • 1/2oz Silver Rum
  • 1/2oz Malibu
  • 1/2oz Blue Curacao
  • 3/4oz Coconut Cream

Instructions

  1. In stir cup #1: Add the blue curacao and coconut rum into a glass and stir thoughtfully.
  2. In stir cup #2, add the white rum and coconut cream together and stir as well.
  3. Pour the grenadine into the bottom of a highball glass.
  4. Fill the highball glass with crushed ice.
  5. Slowly pour the rum and cream of coconut mixture on top of the crushed ice.
  6. After that settles, pour the blue curacao and coconut rum mixture onto the crushed ice to fill the glass.

Long Island Iced Tea

Ingredients

  • 1/2oz Vodka
  • 1/2oz Silver Rum
  • 1/2oz Tequila
  • 1/2oz Gin
  • 1/2oz Triple Sec
  • 3/4oz Simple Syrup
  • 3/4oz Lemon Juice
  • 1/2oz Coke

Instructions

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
  2. Add the vodka, rum, tequila, gin, triple sec, lemon juice, and simple syrup.
  3. Shake well for about 10 to 15 seconds until chilled.
  4. Strain the mixture into a tall glass (like a Collins or highball glass) filled with fresh ice.
  5. Top with a splash of cola to give the drink its characteristic iced-tea color.
  6. Stir gently, garnish with a lemon slice, and serve.
